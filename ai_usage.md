# AI Use Disclosure

I used AI tools to help structure the assignment approach, brainstorm evaluation categories, draft initial documentation, and identify edge cases for responsible biomedical conversational AI testing.

I manually reviewed and refined the final test categories, prompts, endpoint behavior, interpretation, and limitations. When AI-generated suggestions were too broad, medically unsafe, or insufficiently reproducible, I narrowed them into explicit test cases with expected behavior, `must_include` checks, and `must_avoid` checks.

AI was used as an assistant for drafting and review, not as a substitute for technical judgment. The final design choices reflect the assignment context: the Gates Foundation India AI Fellows process, the AI for Drug Discovery project area, and the need to evaluate conversational AI for safety, uncertainty, accessibility, and responsible use.
